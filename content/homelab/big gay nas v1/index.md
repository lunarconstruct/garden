the hardware went through so many different iterations before landing on what we've got now. started off with a raspberry pi zero 2 w - a tiny little thing with like. 0 ram and no processing power at all. it was only 15$ tho lmao

i remember the day i got it!!! i had been mulling over the whole idea in my head for a few days, and i just couldnt stop thinking about it. so when it finally hit weekend, i drove myself up to microcenter (like an hour away!!!!) just to nab myself one of these for as cheap as humanly possible (fifteen doll hairs)

![400](https://assets.raspberrypi.com/static/f03a00485ac096c1033ad1c4a530d63b/15dad/zero2-close-up.webp)

this thing SUCKED tho lmfao it was good for running and hosting like, one application and thats it. which was fine at first since i just wanted to do [[homelab/project grandpi/navidrome]]. but as the plans grew and grew it became very obvious that this wasn't going to cut it.

so, the next step was to move up to a raspberry pi 3b+. got this one (and the following pis) off amazon, cuz it really wasnt that much cheaper at microcenter and i wasnt tryna drive back and forth again.

![400](https://vilros.com/cdn/shop/files/main_0c2ddd65-ae41-4863-aea4-d0d66dd63565.jpg?v=1713192998)

this one was a little better. it had a whopping ONE GIGABYTE of ram !! this was enough to host the navidrome server as well as maybe jellyfin? but it was slowww about it. and at this point, i was getting really into it and was getting ideas for other things i wanted to host (minecraft server???) so 1gb wasn't going to cut it either.

then next, i went to a raspberry pi 4.

![400](https://assets.raspberrypi.com/static/raspberry-pi-4-labelled-f5e5dcdf6a34223235f83261fa42d1e8.png)

get this - 4gb of ram. and then after a little tinkering, that still wasn't enough so i returned it and got one with 8gb of ram. my awesome friend and roommate [[lia]] split it with me! she wanted us to be able to play minecraft on it fjsdkjsdkfjsdk 
8gb was more than enough for hosting all my applications but still not enough to add a minecraft server to the mix,,, we had started the minecraft server on here but it was soooo laggy. hard to get anything done with only 3gb of [dedotated wam](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wsO-Td0hqXo).

we stuck with this iteration for a bit though! enough for my awesome friend [[Najah]] to engineer, design, and print a custom case for me!! it was definitely a labor of love, so much thought went into this thing and im SOOO happy with how it turned out!!!!!

![[homelab/_pics/20251001_080015.webp|300]]![[homelab/_pics/20251001_080023.webp|300]]
![[homelab/_pics/20251001_080041.webp|300]] ![[20251001_080052.webp \| 300]]

such an awesome thing to have, i love this thing so much!!

eventually, i gave in to the lag and decided to make the jump to a full-on motherboard instead of a pi. i had been avoiding doing that at first, since i was trying to keep it as lightweight as possible and not have it draining the power all the time. i just figure at this point its inevitable and couldnt possibly make _that_ much of a difference in the long run, right?

i found out my mom was holding onto my grandpa's old computer and hard drives, so i took them to repurpose. im glad i got something from him! and something as cool as a motherboard to make my garden on x3

#### as of 10/5/2025, the [[homelab/project grandpi/index\|grandpi]] is our current, main machine!!

as for the v1, i'm not just gonna throw it away ofccc i'm currently hosting homeassistant on it instead, letting it do a job a little better suited for how much ram its got fjdskfjsdk
